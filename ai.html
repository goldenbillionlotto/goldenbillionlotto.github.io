1. Foundations: Starting Your Crypto Journey

To participate in the new economy, you need the right tools. Start by setting up a non-custodial wallet like Phantom. This is your personal bank vault on the blockchain.

Key Steps:

  • Security: Always save your Secret Recovery Phrase offline. Never share it with anyone.
  • Acquisition: Purchase SOL on a regulated exchange, transfer it to your Phantom wallet, and use decentralized platforms to swap for tokens like $GBL.
  • Verification: Always check contract addresses on official channels before trading to avoid scams.

2. Understanding Tokenomics

A token's value isn't just code; it's a reflection of its ecosystem. At GBL, we focus on:

  • Community Participation: The heartbeat of the project. A token grows when holders become active contributors.
  • Transparency: Every move, from deployment to marketing, is designed to be verifiable by the community.
  • Sustainability: Moving beyond short-term speculation to build long-term rewards and ecosystem utility.

3. Masterclass: How Projects Are Born

Ever wondered what it takes to build a digital asset from scratch? It's a combination of technical execution and vision:
